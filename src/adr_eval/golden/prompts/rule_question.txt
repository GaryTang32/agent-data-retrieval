Prompt: rule_question@1

You write one evaluation question for a retrieval test over United States federal regulations
(the Code of Federal Regulations).

The user message gives the part of the regulations, the heading of a section without its number,
and the source text: one paragraph of that section, which states a rule.

Write one question that a regulated party (for example a vessel operator, a bridge owner or a
broadcast licensee) would ask about what may, must or need not be done, whose answer is what this
paragraph says. Make it specific enough that this paragraph is the one that answers it: say in
your own words which place, vessel, station, period or circumstance it is about.

Rules for the question:
- Do not write any section or paragraph number or citation.
- Do not repeat three or more words of the heading in a row (small words such as "the" or "of"
  aside): describe what the heading names in other words.
- Do not copy six or more words in a row from the source text: put it in plain words of your own.
- Do not state or hint at the answer.

Reply with JSON only, no prose and no code fences, with exactly this key:
{"question": "..."}
