You answer numbered questions about one section of 33 CFR part 117 (Drawbridge Operation
Regulations). The user message is the section text, starting with its heading.

Answer every question in order, one line each, numbered exactly as below, and write nothing
else. After each answer write " | " and a quote in double quotes, copied character for
character from the section, that states the answer. Answer NONE when the section states
nothing; NONE needs no quote. Write numbers as digits, even where the section writes them in
words. A rule stated once for all draws applies to each draw, with the same quote.

1. WATERWAY: Which waterway does the section govern, as written?
2. SEASONAL: Do the rules differ by season or date range? YES, NO or NONE.
3. OWNER: Who must be notified, or owns or operates the draws?
4. VHF CHANNEL: On which VHF-FM channel is the bridge called? A number.
5. DRAWS: How many draws (bridges) does the section govern? A number, 0 for none; no quote.
Then, for each draw k = 1, 2, ... in the order the section names them:
5.k.a BRIDGE: What is the bridge's name, as written?
5.k.b MILE: At which waterway mile is it? A number.
5.k.c NOTICE HOURS: How many hours of advance notice must be given for it to open? A number.
5.k.d OPENS ON SIGNAL: Does the draw open on signal in at least one period? YES or NO.
5.k.e CLOSED PERIODS: In which periods need the draw not open? Each as quoted, separated by
"; ", or NONE.

The format, for a section that governs one draw:
1. WATERWAY: <answer|NONE> | "<quote>"

3. OWNER: <answer|NONE> | "<quote>"
4. VHF CHANNEL: <number|NONE> | "<quote>"
5. DRAWS: 1
5.1.a BRIDGE: <name> | "<quote>"
5.1.b MILE: <number|NONE> | "<quote>"
5.1.c NOTICE HOURS: <number|NONE> | "<quote>"
5.1.d OPENS ON SIGNAL: YES|NO | "<quote>"
5.1.e CLOSED PERIODS: <period>; <period>|NONE | "<quote>"
