You read one section of 33 CFR part 117 (Drawbridge Operation Regulations) and give, per rule
card, who the rule binds and its conditions, and, per draw, the parameters of its operating
rule. The user message is the section text, starting with its heading, then a line
"RULE CARDS:" and the provision ids of the section's rule cards, one per line.

Reply with JSON only, no prose and no code fences, in exactly this shape:
{"cards": {"<provision id>": {"subject": <cell>, "conditions": <cell>, "thresholds": <cell>, "exceptions": <cell>}, ...},
 "draws": [{"bridge": <cell>, "mile": <cell>, "opening_basis": <cell>, "notice_hours": <cell>,
            "periods": [{"effect": <cell>, "start": <cell>, "end": <cell>, "days": <cell>, "except_federal_holidays": <cell>, "date_from": <cell>, "date_to": <cell>}, ...]}, ...]}

A cell is {"value": <value or null>, "quote": "<text from the section>" or null, "null_reason": null, "absent" or "not_applicable"}.
- "quote" is copied character for character from the section text: the shortest phrase or
  sentence that states the value. Never paraphrase, abbreviate, or join separate passages.
  For a list give one quote that holds every item of the list.
- Write numbers as digits, even where the section writes them in words ("four hours notice"
  is 4).
- When the section states nothing for a cell, the cell is {"value": null, "quote": null,
  "null_reason": "absent"}; use "not_applicable" when the cell cannot apply.
- A negative or false value needs a quote that states it, like every other value: false for
  except_federal_holidays only when the section says the period holds on holidays (for
  example "including Federal holidays"), 0 for notice_hours only when the section says no
  notice is needed, "closed_to_navigation" only when the section says the draw is closed. A
  negative that would rest on the section saying nothing is {"value": null, "quote": null,
  "null_reason": "absent"}. Never infer one.

Cards: one entry under "cards" per provision id listed under RULE CARDS, keyed by that id.
- subject (a string): who or what the rule binds, as the paragraph names it.
- conditions (a list of strings): when the rule applies, as written: times, days, dates,
  holidays, notice, vessels, signals.
- thresholds (a list of strings): the numeric thresholds, as written ("at least four hours
  notice").
- exceptions (a list of strings): the cases the rule sets aside, as written.

Draws: one object under "draws" per draw (bridge) the section governs, in the order the
section names them; "draws" is [] when the section governs no particular draw. A rule stated
once for all draws of the section is repeated on each draw, with the same quote.
- bridge (a string): the bridge's name, as written.
- mile (a number): the waterway mile of the bridge.
- opening_basis: how the draw opens where no period below says otherwise: "on_signal",
  "advance_notice" (it opens only after advance notice) or "closed_to_navigation". It applies
  at all other times: wherever none of the draw's periods applies (at all times when the draw
  has no period). "At all other times, the draw shall open on signal if at least 24 hours
  notice is given" is "advance_notice" with notice_hours 24.
- notice_hours (an integer): the hours of advance notice an opening needs. A notice that holds
  only in some window is a "notice_required" period; a notice with no "notice_required"
  period, or with "advance_notice", applies at all other times too: wherever none of the
  draw's periods applies.
- periods: each time window that has its own rule, as stated:
  - effect: "need_not_open" (the draw need not open, or stays closed, in the window),
    "on_signal" (it opens on signal in the window) or "notice_required" (it opens in the
    window only after the notice in notice_hours);
  - start and end: clock times as written ("7 a.m.", "4:30 p.m.", "noon", "midnight"). Quote
    the whole time range for both ("from 7 a.m. to 9 a.m."). Both are null when the period
    holds all day;
  - days: the weekdays it holds on, a list of names (["Monday", "Tuesday", "Wednesday",
    "Thursday", "Friday"]); null when it holds every day;
  - except_federal_holidays: true when the period does not hold on Federal holidays;
  - date_from and date_to: the first and the last day of its date range, as written
    ("April 1", "October 31"); null when it holds all year.
  Give one period per time range: "from 7 a.m. to 9 a.m. and from 4 p.m. to 6 p.m., Monday
  through Friday" is two periods with the same days.
