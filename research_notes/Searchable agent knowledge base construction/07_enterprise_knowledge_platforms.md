# Enterprise knowledge platforms and case studies: how commercial products turn company documents, KB articles, policies and SOPs into knowledge bases that AI agents search and follow

Scope: commercial products and real deployments, current as of November 2026. Open-source stacks are covered in another track. Dates are given for anything older than 2026. "Vendor claim" marks figures a vendor published itself without independent audit. Earlier notes (competitive_landscape_gaps.md) already covered: Glean's acquisition of Aryn, the Bedrock GraphRAG limits, and Azure/Databricks/Snowflake KB products. Those are not repeated here.

## 1. Enterprise search and knowledge assistants: how each product ingests, represents, retrieves and permissions knowledge

### Takeaway
Every enterprise knowledge assistant uses the same three layers. (1) Connectors crawl content, and also ACLs and identities, into a hybrid lexical-plus-vector index. (2) An "activity/people graph" adds ranking signals. (3) Permissions are enforced again at query time. In 2025–26 each vendor also added a second, "federated/real-time" path (live API or MCP calls with the user's own token) for content that must not be copied. The products differ mainly in freshness guarantees, how strictly they handle ACLs (fail-closed vs. stale), and how they charge (seats, credits or tokens). They do not differ much in retrieval technique.

### Cited Findings

**Glean**
- The knowledge graph stores entities and relationships as triples, for example (Engineer A, owns, Jira Ticket B). Edges carry timestamps, access control, confidence and provenance. LLMs are used to grow the graph to "thousands of edges." A "personal graph" groups each user's atomic actions into tasks, projects and themes. A "real-time crawler architecture" feeds lexical search, semantic search and the graph, and normalizes each source into a source-agnostic schema. (Article dated June 2025.) — [Glean blog: knowledge graph](https://www.glean.com/blog/knowledge-graph-agentic-engine)
- Connectors run in three modes. *Indexed* (crawled, webhook-driven "near real-time" updates, searchable org-wide subject to permissions). *Real-time access* (fetched live at query time, nothing stored, per-user delegated auth). *Read/write tools* for agents. Permissions are read from the source and enforced at query time. — [Glean docs: OneDrive connector](https://docs.glean.com/connectors/native/onedrive/about)
- Glean Protect covers indexing controls and one-time sensitive-content reports. Protect+ (licensed separately) adds weekly sensitive-content scans across 100+ sources, continuous scans for some connectors, auto-remediation of "overshared" content in AI surfaces, and AI guardrails against prompt injection and jailbreaks, plus pre-execution tool-alignment checks (beta). — [Glean docs: Protect overview](https://docs.glean.com/administration/protect/overview)
- An independent (but competing) security vendor argues that permission-aware RAG still leaks. LLMs can combine individually permitted fragments from Slack, Confluence and email into sensitive inferences. Permissions encode "can access," not "need to know." (July 2025, vendor view.) — [Knostic blog](https://www.knostic.ai/blog/glean-data-security)

**Microsoft 365 Copilot + SharePoint**
- The semantic index is generated automatically from Microsoft Graph content. It cannot be disabled and needs no admin setup. There is a tenant-level index built from text-based SharePoint Online files, and a user-level index built from the mailbox and the user's working set. It covers docx, pptx, pdf, aspx, OneNote and Copilot-connector data, with files up to 512 MB. Results surface only if the user already has access, and the site must be searchable. Freshness: mailbox content is indexed near real-time. New SharePoint documents shared with two or more users are indexed **daily**. Updates to already-indexed documents are indexed "immediately." The only exclusions are site-level "not searchable" settings or Purview DLP. Excluding a site removes it from both Microsoft Search and the semantic index. (Page updated August 2026.) — [Microsoft Learn: semantic indexing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftsearch/semantic-index-for-copilot)
- When a user scopes a Copilot query to a SharePoint library or folder, the library's **column metadata** is used as a grounding and ranking signal. — [Microsoft Learn: semantic indexing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftsearch/semantic-index-for-copilot)
- Copilot connectors (formerly Graph connectors) come in two models. *Synced* connectors push content into Graph using an `externalItem` schema with item-level ACLs, and are semantically indexed on **title and content only**. *Federated* connectors use MCP to fetch at query time, with no indexing. Semantic labels are for filtering and do not affect semantic indexing. Microsoft says semantic indexing does **not** help queries with no topic ("find bugs assigned to"), multi-parameter queries (topic plus assignee), or count queries. There are 100+ gallery connectors. Microsoft's guidance is to put rich text in `content`, add `urlToItemResolver` and user activities, and describe the connection well. A sample "policies connector" ingests local policy files and maps front-matter metadata to the schema. (September 2026.) — [Microsoft Learn: Copilot connectors overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/extensibility/overview-copilot-connector)
- Microsoft's 2026 deployment blueprint starts with "remediate oversharing," using SharePoint Advanced Management (included with Copilot) and Purview, before guardrails and regulatory steps. — [Microsoft Learn: Secure & governed foundation blueprint](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/plan/secure-govern-copilot-foundational-deployment-guidance)

**Google Gemini Enterprise (formerly Agentspace)**
- Launched on October 9, 2025 as the successor positioning to Agentspace. Components: models, a no-code agent builder, prebuilt agents, enterprise data integration, governance, and partners. — [Techzine](https://www.techzine.eu/news/applications/135293/google-launches-gemini-enterprise-the-front-door-for-workplace-ai)
- Connectors offer a choice between *data ingestion* (copied into the index, which Google says gives better quality) and *data federation* (queried live, which Google says may lower quality). Each entity type gets its own data store (for example Jira issues, comments, attachments), classed as structured or unstructured. Sync is split into *entity* sync and *identity* sync (users and groups mapped to ACLs). Schedules: full sync every 3h to 3 days; identity sync every 30 min to 7 days; incremental sync every 3h to 7 days, default 3h. Incremental sync does **not** capture deletions or identity changes, so a periodic full sync is required. Throughput example: at 5 QPS a one-day sync pulls about 432k records. — [Google Cloud docs: connectors and data stores](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/connectors/introduction-to-connectors-and-data-stores)

**AWS: Amazon Q Business, Quick Suite, Bedrock Knowledge Bases**
- Amazon Q Business is "no longer open to new customers." AWS points to Amazon Quick as the successor. — [AWS Q Business docs](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/amazonq/latest/qbusiness-ug/connector-concepts.md)
- Q Business connectors index document ACLs (user email, local group, federated group) into a "User Store." A separate *identity crawler* maps local IDs to federated identities. ACL changes are picked up only on re-crawl. Once ACL and identity crawling are enabled they **cannot be turned off**. Documents without ACLs are treated as public. Sync modes: full, new/modified, new/modified/deleted, or change log. Schedules: on demand, hourly, daily, weekly, monthly or cron. A "document deletion safeguard" (a maximum deletion percentage) stops crawler failures from wiping the index. Metadata field mappings are limited to 2,048 characters per attribute. AWS warns that editing the User Store is a privileged operation that can silently change document visibility. — [AWS Q Business docs: connector concepts](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/amazonq/latest/qbusiness-ug/connector-concepts.md)
- Bedrock Knowledge Bases is a managed RAG pipeline: parse, chunk, embed, write to a vector store, then Retrieve or RetrieveAndGenerate. Chunking options: default (~300 tokens, sentence-preserving), fixed-size with overlap, hierarchical (child chunks retrieved, parent chunks returned), semantic (buffer size plus breakpoint percentile; uses an FM at extra cost), or none (pre-split files or use a custom Lambda). For markdown-parsed content containing tables or figures, the matched chunk is replaced with the full page or table (up to 10,000 characters), so fewer results than `numberOfResults` may come back. — [AWS docs: KB chunking](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/kb-chunking.html); [AWS docs: how KBs work](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/kb-how-it-works.html)
- Bedrock KBs have **no native source-ACL sync**. AWS's own pattern is per-document `*.metadata.json` sidecar files plus a metadata filter at retrieve time (July 2024). — [AWS ML blog: metadata filtering access control](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/access-control-for-vector-stores-using-metadata-filtering-with-knowledge-bases-for-amazon-bedrock) The AWS Security Blog (September 2025) says metadata filtering is only a "baseline filtering capability." Vector stores sync periodically, so permission changes lag, and principals in hundreds of groups are hard to filter. It recommends checking each retrieved chunk against the source's authorization (S3 Access Grants example) and treating the LLM as untrusted. — [AWS Security Blog: authorizing access in RAG](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/security/authorizing-access-to-data-with-rag-implementations)

**Atlassian Rovo (Confluence/Jira)**
- Teamwork Graph connectors sync content and per-document permissions from third-party apps. Each connector has a "freshness window." If permission updates are delayed, incomplete or failing, **Rovo hides that connector's results until sync recovers** (fail-closed). Salesforce is the exception: permissions are validated per query. — [Atlassian support: connector permission sync](https://support.atlassian.com/organization-administration/docs/how-connector-permissions-are-kept-in-sync/)
- Rovo Search, Definitions and Summaries are free. Chat, agents and cross-product "enriched" Teamwork Graph/MCP queries consume credits (1–10 credits per enriched call; 10 per basic chat message). — [Atlassian support: Rovo credits](https://support.atlassian.com/organization-administration/docs/usage-limits-in-atlassian-intelligence/)

**Notion AI**
- Connectors cover Slack, Teams, Google Drive, SharePoint/OneDrive, Jira (beta), GitHub, Linear, Gmail, Outlook and Google Calendar. Only about **one year of history** from the setup date is ingested. Initial ingestion takes up to 36h. New content takes up to 3h to appear. Disconnected content becomes unsearchable within about 1h and is deleted within a day. Permission mapping is per app. Notion says it is best for finding and summarizing, not aggregation or calculation. Business/Enterprise plans only. — [Notion help: AI connectors](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-connectors)

**Guru (verified-knowledge layer)**
- Every Guru Card has a verifier (a person or group) and a verification interval (30d to 1y, or "does not expire"). An edit by a non-verifier un-verifies the card. "Knowledge Agents" review connected cards and external sources daily (7-day cooldown) using behavioral rules (votes, views, expert feedback) and content rules (AI checks for dates, time-sensitivity, near-duplicates). Unverify rules run before verify rules. Agents can be restricted to "verified only," "verified + no status," or "all sources." Auto-verify is on by default for agents created after January 2026. — [Guru help: verification](https://help.getguru.com/docs/verifying-and-unverifying-cards)
- Guru's MCP server exposes Knowledge Agents, search and research with citations, lineage and permission-aware answers. Cards drafted through MCP go through the human review workflow. All MCP calls are logged in an "AI Agent Center." Guru positions itself as an "AI Source of Truth" for ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor and Copilot (vendor claim). — [Guru: MCP server](https://www.getguru.com/features/mcp-server)

**Coveo**
- A unified hybrid (full-text plus semantic) index. The Passage Retrieval API is two-stage: rank documents, then extract passages with `relevanceScore`, citations and metadata. It is offered as headless retrieval for Copilot, Agentforce and Bedrock. Coveo says permissions are enforced at indexing time with "real-time security trimming." No accuracy numbers are published. (July 2025.) — [Coveo blog](https://www.coveo.com/blog/ms-copilot-coveo/)

**Elastic**
- Agent Builder was a technical preview in October 2025 and went GA in January 2026. It has a built-in agent that picks indices and writes semantic, hybrid or structured queries; custom ES|QL tools; per-agent security profiles; and MCP and A2A endpoints. "Elastic Workflows" (deterministic automation) is in tech preview. — [Techzine: Agent Builder launch](https://www.techzine.eu/news/devops/135723/elastic-launches-agent-builder-to-build-ai-agents-faster); [TechIntelPro: Agent Builder GA](https://techintelpro.com/news/ai/enterprise-ai/elastic-agent-builder-ga-build-context-driven-ai-agents-fast)
- The `semantic_text` field embeds content at index time through Elastic Inference Service or ML nodes. Elastic recommends pinning `inference_id` because default endpoints change across versions. — [Elastic docs: semantic_text](https://www.elastic.co/docs/solutions/search/semantic-search/semantic-search-semantic-text)

**Moveworks (now ServiceNow)**
- ServiceNow completed the Moveworks acquisition on December 15, 2025 (price not disclosed in the release). Moveworks reported 5.5M employee users, about 250 mutual customers, nearly 90% of customers deployed to all employees, and 100+ integrations. — [ServiceNow press release](https://newsroom.servicenow.com/press-releases/details/2025/ServiceNow-completes-acquisition-of-Moveworks/default.aspx)
- Moveworks offers *indexed* search (semantic plus keyword with query rephrasing, permissions synced continuously alongside content) and *live* search (the source API is called with the user's credentials). Only results above a relevance threshold are summarized with citations. — [Moveworks help: indexed search](https://help.moveworks.com/ai-assistant/enterprise-search/indexed-search) "Agentic RAG" in four steps: understand objective, enrich/plan query, retrieve/rank, summarize. Content recency is weighed as a reliability signal (October 2024). — [Moveworks blog](https://moveworks.com/us/en/resources/blog/introducing-enterprise-search)

**Dashworks (shut down) and Onyx Cloud**
- HubSpot agreed to acquire Dashworks on April 16, 2025. Dashworks described its approach as "real-time agentic search" rather than index-centric. — [Dashworks blog](https://www.dashworks.ai/blog/dashworks-is-joining-forces-with-hubspot) The standalone product was **shut down on July 16, 2025**: the API was cut on April 23, customers got a 90-day export window, and data was deleted within 30 days. — [Vantagepoint](https://vantagepoint.io/blog/hs/what-happened-to-dashworks-ai)
- Onyx (formerly Danswer): permission-sync connectors are an Enterprise Edition feature. "Document Sets" group documents for agents and filters. — [Onyx docs: connectors](https://docs.onyx.app/overview/core_features/connectors) Cloud Business is $20/user/month (annual), with 40+ connectors and "Permission Inheritance." Enterprise (SSO, on-prem) is contact-sales. — [Onyx pricing](https://onyx.app/pricing)

### Inferences
- Across these products, the "knowledge base" is built from ordinary documents plus three side-channels: ACLs/identities, activity signals, and freshness/verification metadata. A toolkit that outputs only chunks plus embeddings would miss the parts enterprises pay for.
- Three ACL designs exist. (a) Sync ACLs at ingest and filter at query (Glean, Q Business, Google, Moveworks, Microsoft). (b) Fail closed when ACL sync is stale (Rovo). (c) Live per-user fetch (federated/MCP modes, Rovo's Salesforce connector). (c) is spreading because it removes ACL lag but costs retrieval quality, as Google states explicitly.
- Microsoft's own admission that vector indexing fails for "assigned to X," multi-filter and count queries supports the earlier track's finding: typed and structured stores must sit next to the searchable KB.

### Gaps
- No vendor publishes retrieval-quality benchmarks (recall/precision) for its enterprise index. Glean, Coveo and Moveworks quality claims are all qualitative.
- Glean's Confluence connector page returned 404, so per-connector crawl intervals for Glean could not be verified beyond "near real-time via webhooks."
- Gemini Enterprise current per-seat prices and ACL edge cases (nested groups) were not verified on an official page.

## 2. Customer-service knowledge and SOP-driven agents: how SOPs and policies are authored and enforced

### Takeaway
CS-agent vendors have converged on a split. **Knowledge** (articles, snippets, PDFs, synced help centers) is retrieved by RAG for informational answers. **Procedures/SOPs** are authored as natural-language steps with deterministic code or conditions for policy-critical checks, then tested with simulated conversations. Salesforce (Agent Script), Intercom/Fin (Procedures), Decagon (AOPs), Sierra (Journeys/Agent SDK) and Ada (Playbooks) all follow this split. The shared lesson: "if it can be code, make it code." Policies must not live only as prose the LLM may ignore.

### Cited Findings

**Salesforce Agentforce**
- The Agentforce Data Library grounds agents in knowledge articles, uploaded files, websites and conversation transcripts. Setup auto-creates a Data Cloud data stream, then data lake and model objects, then chunks, a search index and a retriever (one per library). At runtime a prompt template calls the retriever, and the response is checked against instructions before display. — [Trailhead: Data Library basics](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/agentforce-data-library-basics/upgrade-ai-with-real-world-data)
- A practitioner build (October 2026) shows the lower-level path: web crawler into a DLO, then a DMO, then a search index, then an Individual Retriever (20 results, returning `Chunk__c`), then a Flex prompt template based on "Answer Questions with Knowledge" returning JSON with `source_id`, then a subagent whose instructions restrict answers to retrieved articles. Validation was a single manual test. — [SalesforceBreak](https://salesforcebreak.com/2026/10/22/agentic-rag-data-cloud-agentforce-studio/)
- Agent Script and hybrid reasoning went GA in February 2026. "Topics" were renamed "subagents." Design rules: "if you can express a decision as code, it should be written as logic." Business rules become thresholds and conditions evaluated on every turn. `available when` gates remove actions from the LLM's tool list until conditions hold. Gate variables stay under deterministic control and start closed. Auth and entitlement checks run in `before_reasoning`. The LLM is reserved for intent, ambiguity and empathetic wording. Groundedness validation is one of the LLM call points. — [Salesforce Architects: hybrid reasoning](https://architect.salesforce.com/docs/architect/fundamentals/guide/hybrid-reasoning-agentforce-builder-agent-script)
- Salesforce agreed to acquire Fin (formerly Intercom) for about $3.6B on June 15, 2026. Close is expected in Salesforce's FY2027 Q4. Fin claims 30,000+ customers and an average 76% automated resolution (self-reported). — [Trending Topics](https://www.trendingtopics.eu/salesforce-to-acquire-fin-ai-formerly-intercom-for-3-6-billion/)

**Intercom / Fin**
- Content sources: public and internal articles, snippets, website sync (weekly re-crawl, manual re-sync), external syncs (Zendesk, Guru, Notion, Confluence, Salesforce, Box, Freshdesk, Document360), and PDFs (private, never linked to customers). **Audiences** restrict which content Fin may use per customer segment. Intercom recommends phrasing headings and opening paragraphs in customer language. — [Intercom help: content sources](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/7837514-add-content-sources-for-fin-ai-agent)
- Procedures: natural-language steps, AI-drafted from an outline, plus conditions in natural language **or Python** (not mixed within one step, no nesting), code steps for eligibility and date math, sub-procedures, and data connectors/MCP with engineer-controlled response fields (PII excluded). Fin triggers a procedure on intent; event and inbox triggers also exist. Steps run sequentially only. "Simulations" run an AI customer through a whole conversation against pass/fail criteria and can be re-run as regression tests. Intercom says to use articles/snippets for simple FAQs, not procedures. — [Fin help: Procedures explained](https://fin.ai/help/en/articles/13976187-fin-procedures-explained)
- "Guidance" (style, clarification, spam behavior) is a third layer next to content and procedures, with reporting on adherence. — [Intercom help: improve Fin resolution rate](https://intercom.com/help/en/articles/9790492-how-to-improve-fin-s-resolution-rate)

**Decagon (AOPs)**
- Agent Operating Procedures are "natural language instructions that compile into structured logic." Converting an SOP means splitting it into steps with triggers and actions, required data points, decision branches, success criteria, escalation conditions, and validation for sensitive actions. CX teams write the prose, and engineers add code guardrails and integrations. Deterministic code runs refunds and account changes. Every change is versioned, and each execution leaves a step-by-step trace. Existing SOPs, scripts, KB articles and policy documents are the source material, audited first. Vendor-reported example: NG.CASH reached 70% deflection. (November 2025.) — [Decagon: from SOPs to AOPs](https://decagon.ai/blog/from-sops-to-agent-operating-procedures)

**Sierra**
- Agent Studio (September 2025): knowledge comes from connected help centers, internal docs, JSON-imported articles, or articles written in-studio. RAG is tuned for KBs with "hundreds of thousands of articles" (vendor claim). The system flags knowledge gaps. "Journeys" define workflows in natural language with per-channel variants, and are tested with simulations. — [Sierra blog: Agent Studio](https://sierra.ai/blog/meet-agent-studio)
- The Agent SDK sets goals and guardrails to keep agents within policy. A customer (Ramp) writes "customer journeys as code" and tracks changes in its normal SDLC. — [Sierra: Agent SDK](https://sierra.ai/product/agent-sdk)

**Ada, Kore.ai, ServiceNow, Zendesk**
- Ada's Unified Reasoning Engine (February 18, 2026): one knowledge base and instruction set across channels and languages. "Playbooks" follow SOPs over multi-step calls. "Coaching" learns from feedback. Policies apply the same way on every channel. — [DestinationCRM](https://www.destinationcrm.com/Articles/CRM-News/CRM-Across-the-Wire/Ada-Launches-Unified-Reasoning-Engine-173555.aspx)
- Kore.ai Search AI exposes chunking as configurable "extraction strategies." Text extraction works by page or by tokens (up to 5,000 tokens). Layout-aware extraction (OCR plus layout detection) handles tables and charts. Advanced HTML has a General template and a Token template (100–1,000 tokens, default 300). Markdown extraction is the default for PDF/DOC/DOCX, image-based uses VDR embeddings, custom uses a callback API, and there is a JSON mode. Defaults apply to apps created after November 19, 2025. — [Kore.ai docs: extraction](https://docs.kore.ai/xo/searchai/content-extraction/extraction/)
- ServiceNow Now Assist "KB generation" drafts an article from a resolved case, incident or work-order task. Fields used: short description, description, resolution notes, close notes, work notes, comments. Agents review and publish (Xanadu, August 2024). — [ServiceNow docs: KB generation](https://www.servicenow.com/docs/r/x7YYxCVARboMBfN2zKAZsA/_CSz4yfJG8Svh8O92KusJg) It shipped in 2Q24 (Washington DC P3). A 3Q24 update generates one article from a group of similar incidents. — [ServiceNow community: install guide](https://www.servicenow.com/community/itsm-articles/install-and-configure-knowledge-generation-for-now-assist-for/ta-p/2949753)
- Zendesk Knowledge Builder scans the past **90 days of solved tickets**, clusters common issues, proposes a help-center hierarchy (up to 10 regenerations), and writes articles as drafts labeled "AI-generated" for admin review. It is GA. — [Zendesk blog: Knowledge Builder](https://www.zendesk.com/blog/zendesk-insights/innovation/fuel-your-ai-with-a-knowledge-base-built-in-days-not-months/)

### Inferences
- The common SOP representation has five parts: (1) a trigger or intent description, (2) natural-language steps, (3) typed slots or data points, (4) deterministic guards (code or conditions) for eligibility, amounts and authorization, (5) escalation and success criteria. Each procedure also carries a version history and a regression test suite (simulations). This is far more structured than a "policy PDF chunk." A toolkit that wants agents to *follow* SOPs should extract this structure, not just index the text.
- Vendors deliberately keep FAQs as retrievable content and procedures as executable artifacts. Each layer has its own lifecycle (content hygiene for one, versioning and tests for the other).

### Gaps
- No vendor documents how its "compile to logic" step works internally (Decagon, Sierra). Public material is marketing-level.
- No independent comparison of SOP-adherence rates across Agentforce, Fin, Decagon and Sierra was found.
- Salesforce developer docs (developer.salesforce.com) returned 403, so exact Data Library chunking defaults (reported in search snippets as section-aware, 1,200 tokens, 0 overlap, for website content) are unverified.

## 3. Ingestion details: connectors, chunking, metadata, ACL sync, freshness, content-gap detection, article generation

### Takeaway
Freshness ranges from near-real-time webhooks (Glean, mailbox) to daily (new SharePoint files in the semantic index) to weekly (Fin website sync) to multi-hour schedules (Google default 3h incremental). ACL/identity sync often runs on a separate, faster or slower cadence than content. The newest knowledge-ops feature is the closed loop: mine failed or escalated conversations and solved tickets, cluster them, and draft or edit articles behind a human-approval gate. Fin, Decagon, Zendesk, ServiceNow, Sierra and Guru all ship a version of this.

### Cited Findings

- **Content-gap detection from conversations (Fin).** Recommendations compare failed Fin answers (escalations, poor replies) against successful human replies to similar questions, and also mine teammate-only conversations. Create/edit suggestions run daily or weekly depending on volume, recurring topics (≥7 days) and query spikes. Duplicate and contradiction scans run every Sunday (up to 20 suggestions). Excluded: conversations with no teammate reply, abandoned conversations, bug or feature requests, and cases where the teammate repeated Fin's answer. Only users with publish rights can accept. Accepted snippets go live immediately, while article edits can stay drafts. Pending suggestions expire after 4 weeks. Requires the Pro add-on. — [Intercom help: AI suggestions](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/11394959-use-ai-powered-suggestions-to-improve-fin)
- Fin content analytics: "involved" vs. "resolved" counts per content item. High-involved/low-resolved items flag content that is missing, inaccurate or ambiguous. Snippets are written as quick fixes and later merged into articles. — [Intercom help: improve resolution rate](https://intercom.com/help/en/articles/9790492-how-to-improve-fin-s-resolution-rate)
- Decagon Suggestions ranks gaps by number of affected conversations and drafts articles "based on how the company's best human agents resolved the same issues." It refreshes monthly, nothing auto-publishes, and drafts can be exported as CSV. — [Decagon: Suggestions](https://decagon.ai/product/suggestions)
- Ticket-to-article generation: Zendesk (90 days of solved tickets, drafts labeled "AI-generated") — [Zendesk blog](https://www.zendesk.com/blog/zendesk-insights/innovation/fuel-your-ai-with-a-knowledge-base-built-in-days-not-months/); ServiceNow (resolution and close notes into a KCS article) — [ServiceNow docs](https://www.servicenow.com/docs/r/x7YYxCVARboMBfN2zKAZsA/_CSz4yfJG8Svh8O92KusJg).
- Garbage in: ServiceNow's own AI CoE notes that incidents closed with "Fixed it" and empty resolution notes starve knowledge generation. It recommends 200+ character descriptions and blocking closure without resolution notes. It reports outcomes qualitatively only ("Partial data produces incomplete knowledge that agents don't trust"; February 2026). — [ServiceNow community: closure notes](https://www.servicenow.com/community/ceg-ai-coe-articles/why-your-itsm-agents-are-closing-tickets-fast-and-teaching-your/ta-p/3484316)
- Verification metadata as a retrieval filter: Guru agents can be limited to verified content, and daily AI re-verification flags stale or duplicate cards. — [Guru help](https://help.getguru.com/docs/verifying-and-unverifying-cards)
- Deletion safety: Q Business's deletion safeguard skips the delete phase when deletions exceed a set percentage, which protects against crawler outages. — [AWS docs](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/amazonq/latest/qbusiness-ug/connector-concepts.md)
- Metadata as ranking signal: SharePoint column metadata is used when a query is scoped to a library. — [MS Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftsearch/semantic-index-for-copilot) Copilot connectors use semantic labels (title, url, iconUrl) and user activities for ranking. — [MS Learn: connectors](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/extensibility/overview-copilot-connector)

### Inferences
- A practical "knowledge-ops loop" for an open toolkit has four stages: (1) ingest with ACL plus freshness metadata, (2) log which chunks were "involved" vs. "resolved," (3) cluster failed queries against human resolutions to propose new or edited KB items, (4) run contradiction and duplicate detection over the corpus on a schedule, with every write going through human approval. Commercial products show that each stage is feasible and valued.
- Separate cadences for identity sync and content sync, plus fail-closed behavior on stale ACLs, are cheap design choices that prevent the worst leak class.

### Gaps
- No product publishes measured gap-detection precision (how many suggestions are accepted).
- Coveo, Glean and Moveworks do not document chunk sizes or strategies publicly.

## 4. Case studies with numbers, practitioner complaints, incidents and legal/compliance lessons

### Takeaway
Vendor case studies report large but self-measured gains: 70–85% autonomous resolution in CS, and thousands of hours saved per month in enterprise search. Independent evaluations are much more modest. The UK government found self-reported time savings, and DBT found no evidence of productivity gains. The common failure modes are documented repeatedly: stale or conflicting content producing confident wrong policy answers (Air Canada, Cursor, NYC MyCity, Salesforce's own "content collisions"), oversharing permissions delaying deployments (Gartner 40%, CoreView 66%), and prompt-injection exfiltration across trust boundaries in RAG (EchoLeak). The legal baseline is set: a company is liable for what its bot says.

### Cited Findings

**Vendor-reported outcomes (treat as vendor claims)**
- Glean Forrester TEI (commissioned by Glean, September 2024): composite of 10,000 employees with 8 repositories (2 needing custom integrations). Up to 110 hours per employee per year of productivity, 36 hours faster onboarding per hire, 20% fewer support requests. Benefits $26.6M vs. costs $11.0M over 3 years, giving NPV $15.6M and ROI 141%. Based on 4 interviewees. — [Forrester TEI of Glean (PDF)](https://tei.forrester.com/go/Glean/workAIplatform/docs/TheTEIofGlean.pdf)
- Glean / Confluent: 15,000+ hours/month saved, 1.5+ hours per employee per week, 13% higher support satisfaction, 20+ tools connected, 70%+ active use. Measurement method not stated. — [Glean customer story: Confluent](https://www.glean.com/resources/customer-stories/confluent)
- Salesforce Help (customer zero): more than 2M conversations since October 2024, with 77% resolution on the Japanese launch (50k+ conversations). Lesson: "a static knowledge base was not enough," so weekly reviews and monitoring were needed (November 2025). — [Salesforce blog](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/support-requests-agentforce/) A July 2025 report gave 84% autonomous resolution, a 5% drop in case volume, and 500 engineers redeployed. — [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/salesforce-used-ai-to-cut-support-load-by-5-but-the-real-win-was-teaching-bots-to-say-im-sorry/)
- Sierra / WeightWatchers: ~70% of cases resolved without a human and CSAT 4.6, reported for the agent's **first week**. — [Sierra customer story](https://sierra.ai/de/customers/weightwatchers)
- Decagon / NG.CASH: 70% deflection after moving off a decision-tree bot. — [Decagon blog](https://decagon.ai/blog/from-sops-to-agent-operating-procedures)

**Measurement critiques**
- CX Today (July 2026) on Salesforce's claim of 70% of 4.3M Help inquiries resolved: no stated period, no audit, "resolved" undefined, and the $2/resolution billing is waived on escalation or negative feedback, so billing does not prove the issue stayed solved. No figures break out intent, reopen rate or repeat contact. Of the cited customer cases, only Telepass (87% without a human, 40k conversations/week) is close to an autonomous claim, and it lacks reopen data. — [CX Today](https://www.cxtoday.com/customer-engagement-journey-orchestration/salesforce-agentforce-contact-center/)
- Fin counts "assumed resolved" (customer left without asking for a human and without negative feedback) alongside "confirmed resolved." — [Intercom help: Fin reporting](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/7837533-fin-ai-agent-reporting)
- Zendesk uses an **LLM to verify** each non-escalated conversation as resolved. Evaluation windows: 72h for email, 2h (configurable to 72h) for messaging. Only verified resolutions are billed. — [Zendesk help: automated resolutions](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/5352026794010)

**Independent evaluations**
- UK cross-government Copilot experiment (20,000 staff, September 30 to December 31, 2024; published June 2025): self-reported 26 min/day saved, no control group. Over 70% said it reduced search time. The report warns Copilot "can expose files a user shouldn't see if access controls are poor." Agents struggled to identify which OneDrive documents they used, which limits traceability. — [GOV.UK findings report](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/microsoft-365-copilot-experiment-cross-government-findings-report/microsoft-365-copilot-experiment-cross-government-findings-report-html)
- UK DBT Copilot pilot (1,000 licences, October–December 2024; reported September 2025): time savings on written tasks, but "no evidence" of productivity gains. Hallucinations were observed. Average use was 1.14 Copilot actions per working day. — [Civil Service World](https://www.civilserviceworld.com/news/article/dbt-trial-finds-copilot-saves-staff-time-but-doesnt-improve-productivity)

**Stale or conflicting knowledge leading to hallucinated policy**
- *Moffatt v. Air Canada*, 2024 BCCRT 149 (February 14, 2024): the chatbot said bereavement fares could be claimed retroactively within 90 days, contradicting the linked policy page. The tribunal rejected Air Canada's argument that the chatbot was a "separate legal entity," found negligent misrepresentation and a duty of care, and asked why customers should have to cross-check parts of the site. Awarded CAD $650.88 plus interest and fees. Lessons drawn: audit bot answers against published policy, and define in vendor contracts what bots may say. — [Manatt](https://manatt.com/insights/newsletters/advertising-law/ai-gone-wild-airline-has-to-honor-a-refund-policy)
- Cursor (April 2025): the support bot "Sam" invented a "one device per subscription" policy. Reddit cancellations followed, a co-founder corrected it publicly, and AI replies were later labeled. — [CX Today](https://www.cxtoday.com/contact-center/a-contact-center-chatbot-invents-company-policies-now-customers-want-out/)
- NYC MyCity (Azure AI, launched October 2023; investigated March 2024): grounded on 2,000+ NYC Business pages, yet it told businesses they could take workers' tips, refuse housing vouchers and go cashless, all illegal. It gave inconsistent answers to the same question and often no source links. — [The Markup](https://themarkup.org/news/2024/03/29/nycs-ai-chatbot-tells-businesses-to-break-the-law)
- Salesforce's own finding: when its team investigated apparent hallucinations, the cause was often **conflicting documents**. One agent used an old, unlinked, rarely updated page that contradicted maintained articles. Salesforce cleaned up duplicates and outdated content and consolidated it. The SDR agent's "I don't know" rate fell from 30% to under 10% (September 2025). — [Salesforce news: customer zero](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/first-year-agentforce-customer-zero/) Many overlapping password-reset articles caused "content collisions." Advice: "Don't over curate your data, but also don't under curate." — [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/salesforce-used-ai-to-cut-support-load-by-5-but-the-real-win-was-teaching-bots-to-say-im-sorry/)
- Gartner (survey of 187 leaders, July–August 2024): 61% of customer service leaders have a backlog of content to update, and over one-third lack formal KB maintenance processes. — [CDO Magazine](https://www.cdomagazine.tech/aiml/85-of-customer-service-leaders-to-explore-genai-solutions-by-2025-gartner-survey)

**Permission leaks and security**
- CoreView survey (279 decision-makers, July 2026): 66% delayed or cancelled Copilot over SharePoint exposure fears, and 73% believe AI already surfaces sensitive information internally. 48% of those delaying are highly concerned about anonymous links, vs. 14% of those deployed. (Vendor survey.) — [CoreView](https://www.coreview.com/news/66-percent-of-enterprises-delay-microsoft-copilot); [Infosecurity Magazine](https://infosecurity-magazine.com/news/microsoft-copilot-delayed-over)
- EchoLeak (CVE-2025-32711, CVSS 9.3, June 2025): a crafted email in the inbox was retrieved by Copilot's RAG alongside privileged data ("LLM scope violation"), and data was exfiltrated via Teams/SharePoint URLs with zero clicks. Fixed server-side, with no known exploitation. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2025/06/zero-click-ai-vulnerability-exposes.html)

**Over-automation reversal**
- Klarna (May 2025): the CEO said the automation push "went too far" and lowered quality, so Klarna resumed hiring human agents. It had earlier claimed its AI did the work of 700 agents and handled 75% of chats. — [Maginative](https://www.maginative.com/article/klarna-dials-back-its-ai-customer-service-strategy-now-its-hiring-humans-again/)
- Gartner (June 2025): over 40% of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by end-2027. Only about 130 of thousands of "agentic" vendors are real ("agent washing"). — [The Star / Reuters](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2025/06/25/over-40-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-scrapped-by-2027-gartner-says)
- Rovo adoption friction (April 2025): customers said agents could not handle chained tasks and that rollout was slow. Forrester's Julie Mohr: customers had "a really tough time going back to executives." — [TechTarget](https://techtarget.com/searchitoperations/news/366622263/Atlassian-Rovo-pricing-shifts-amid-AI-adoption-struggles)

### Inferences
- The most common root cause of "hallucinated policy" in deployed systems is not missing retrieval but **multiple conflicting or stale sources** with no authority ranking (Air Canada page vs. chatbot, Salesforce's unlinked page, duplicated password articles). A KB toolkit should treat "canonical source / effective date / supersedes" metadata and contradiction detection as first-class features.
- Liability law (Moffatt) means policy answers need traceable citations to an authoritative, versioned policy record. It also strengthens the case for deterministic handling of policy-bearing actions (refund eligibility) over free-text generation.
- Resolution-rate metrics are not comparable across vendors (assumed vs. LLM-verified vs. "no escalation"). Any benchmark in the user's toolkit should define resolution explicitly and track reopen/repeat contact.

### Gaps
- No independent audit of any CS-agent vendor's resolution claims was found.
- Primary Gartner pages returned 403, so Gartner figures come via secondary reporting (CDO Magazine, The Star/Reuters). The Gartner "40% of Copilot deployments delayed ≥3 months" figure (2024) appeared only in search snippets and is not cited as verified.
- The Air Canada primary decision page returned 403. Facts come from a law-firm summary.

## 6. Common enterprise implementation pattern for an agent knowledge base, and lessons

### Takeaway
The repeated pattern across vendors and deployments has eight layers. (1) Connector ingest of content **plus ACLs, identities and activity signals**. (2) Format-aware parsing and structure-aware chunking. (3) A hybrid lexical-plus-vector index with metadata (owner, verification, audience, freshness) used for filtering and ranking. (4) Query-time permission trimming, with a federated/live path for sensitive or volatile sources. (5) Retrieval with citations and relevance thresholds. (6) Procedures/SOPs as separate, versioned, test-covered executable artifacts with deterministic guards. (7) A closed-loop knowledge-ops cycle that mines failures into KB edits under human approval. (8) Governance ahead of launch (oversharing remediation, contradiction cleanup, owners and verification cadences). The deployments that failed usually skipped layers 7–8.

### Cited Findings
- Microsoft makes oversharing remediation the first pillar of Copilot deployment. — [Microsoft Learn blueprint](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/plan/secure-govern-copilot-foundational-deployment-guidance) The UK trial advises keeping knowledge-management practices current because poor access controls expose files. — [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/microsoft-365-copilot-experiment-cross-government-findings-report/microsoft-365-copilot-experiment-cross-government-findings-report-html)
- Salesforce started with a limited rollout and targeted content, then added weekly performance reviews and real-time monitoring. Agentforce Observability surfaced knowledge gaps. — [Salesforce blog](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/support-requests-agentforce/)
- Salesforce cleaned up content (removing duplicates and outdated items, consolidating into one location) and curated and validated articles as its core knowledge lesson. — [Salesforce news](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/first-year-agentforce-customer-zero/)
- Deterministic-first policy enforcement: Salesforce Agent Script gates and guard clauses — [Salesforce Architects](https://architect.salesforce.com/docs/architect/fundamentals/guide/hybrid-reasoning-agentforce-builder-agent-script); Fin Python conditions and code steps — [Fin help](https://fin.ai/help/en/articles/13976187-fin-procedures-explained); Decagon deterministic code for refunds and account changes — [Decagon](https://decagon.ai/blog/from-sops-to-agent-operating-procedures).
- Simulation and regression testing of procedures before release: Fin Simulations — [Fin help](https://fin.ai/help/en/articles/13976187-fin-procedures-explained); Sierra simulations — [Sierra](https://sierra.ai/blog/meet-agent-studio).
- Human-approved knowledge generation: Fin suggestions require publish rights — [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/11394959-use-ai-powered-suggestions-to-improve-fin); Zendesk drafts labeled "AI-generated" — [Zendesk](https://www.zendesk.com/blog/zendesk-insights/innovation/fuel-your-ai-with-a-knowledge-base-built-in-days-not-months/); Decagon "nothing goes live automatically" — [Decagon](https://decagon.ai/product/suggestions).
- Verified-only retrieval option and expiring verification as a staleness control. — [Guru](https://help.getguru.com/docs/verifying-and-unverifying-cards)
- Authorization of retrieved chunks against the source of truth before the LLM sees them. — [AWS Security Blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/security/authorizing-access-to-data-with-rag-implementations) Fail-closed on stale permission sync. — [Atlassian](https://support.atlassian.com/organization-administration/docs/how-connector-permissions-are-kept-in-sync/)
- Trust boundaries in context: untrusted inbound content (email) mixed with privileged data enabled EchoLeak. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2025/06/zero-click-ai-vulnerability-exposes.html)
- Customers still expect a human path: Gartner (August 2026) reports 87% of customers say companies using GenAI for service must provide access to a human. This figure came from a search snippet of the Gartner newsroom title, and the page itself was not fetched (403 on Gartner pages). Klarna's reversal supports the point. — [Maginative](https://www.maginative.com/article/klarna-dials-back-its-ai-customer-service-strategy-now-its-hiring-humans-again/)

### Inferences
- Concrete requirements for the user's open toolkit, drawn from the commercial pattern:
  1. **Record schema per knowledge item**: id, source URI, content, chunk lineage (parent/child), title and headings, ACL principals plus an ACL-sync timestamp, owner/verifier, verification status and expiry, effective date / supersedes / canonical flag, audience/segment tags, language, and usage stats (involved/resolved).
  2. **Two knowledge types**: *reference* (articles, policies, snippets, retrieved by hybrid search) and *procedure* (SOP compiled to steps, slots, guards, escalation and tests, executed or followed). Policies that drive actions should have a structured form (thresholds, eligibility rules) next to the prose.
  3. **Connectors with separate content and identity sync**, a deletion safeguard, and fail-closed behavior on stale ACLs. Optionally a federated MCP mode for regulated sources.
  4. **Hygiene jobs**: duplicate and contradiction detection, stale-content flags, and gap mining from agent logs against human resolutions, all producing proposals rather than direct writes.
  5. **Export portability** of the index, ACL map and procedures (the main lock-in point the commercial market shows).
- These requirements are inferred from patterns, not from any single vendor's specification.

### Gaps
- No public, vendor-neutral reference architecture from an analyst (Gartner/Forrester) was accessible. Gartner pages were blocked.
- Little public data exists on ongoing knowledge-ops staffing (for example, how many content FTEs per 1,000 articles) beyond Gartner's note that 58% of leaders plan to upskill agents as knowledge specialists (search snippet only, not fetched).
